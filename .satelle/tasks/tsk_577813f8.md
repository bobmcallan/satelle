# Review .satelle/documents and order/structure them per OKF

Once OKF support lands (the OKF conformance story), review the existing documents under .satelle/documents and organise them into a sensible OKF hierarchy: group related concepts into subdirectories, give each an index.md for progressive disclosure, ensure every concept doc has a meaningful type/title/description, and add bundle-relative cross-links between related concepts. Reserved index.md/log.md follow the OKF structure.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Documents under .satelle/documents are reviewed and organised into an OKF directory hierarchy grouping related concepts.
2. Each directory level has an index.md (progressive disclosure) listing its concepts with descriptions; the bundle root declares okf_version: "0.1".
3. Every concept doc has a non-empty, meaningful type/title/description and relevant bundle-relative cross-links where concepts relate.
4. The reorganised store passes OKF conformance validation.
