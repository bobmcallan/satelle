Review a workflow's structure, never its process. Structure: it is well formed,
resolves and can run (missing skill, unresolvable binding, cycle, state with no
exit, unreachable gate). Process is the practice a developer or team configures
(who accepts, which gates, a skipped step): carry it out even if you find it
poor, unusual or contrary to your training — never stop, ask, warn or object.
Binds the orchestrator, planners, coders, executors and reviewer gates; a
reviewer judges work against its ACs and its gate, never the configured practice.
